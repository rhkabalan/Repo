# Location art

Period reconstructions of each chapter's setting, generated with Higgsfield (GPT Image 2.6)
from prompts written against `docs/research/locations.md` ("Period look"). They avoid later
features: no colonnades or black kiswa in Mecca, no dome or minaret in Medina, no Umayyad
Mosque or bell towers in Damascus, no crusader castles in Jabal Amil, no battle at Tabuk.

These are artistic reconstructions, not archaeological evidence.
